Staff apps privacy notice

NicoNet and NicoMGT · Last updated 7 November 2026

This notice explains how Nicholson Group uses personal information in NicoNet, the staff intranet app, and NicoMGT, the management console app, including their web versions at living.nicholsongroup.co.uk/niconet and living.nicholsongroup.co.uk/nicomgt. It sits alongside the privacy information in your contract of employment or subcontractor agreement and the staff handbook. Residents using the Nicholson Living app should read the Nicholson Living privacy notice.

1. Who we are

The apps are published by Nicholson Lettings Limited (company number 13369529, registered in England and Wales) of 116 Duke Street, Liverpool L1 5JW, ICO registration ZC289932. For your staff records, the controller is the Nicholson Group company that employs or engages you, as named in your contract or agreement. Nicholson Lettings Limited is the controller of resident information handled in NicoMGT.

Questions or requests about your personal information: message the office in NicoNet, write to us at the address above, or email office@nicholsongroup.co.uk with the subject "Privacy".

2. Who the apps are for

NicoNet is for Nicholson Group employees and approved subcontractors. NicoMGT is for Nicholson Group management. You can only sign in with an email address on our staff list. The apps are not intended for anyone under 18.

3. The information we collect

  • Identity and contact details: name, work or personal email address, phone number, role and trade.
  • Clock in and clock out: the time, and one reading of your phone's location at the moment you press Clock in or Clock out, checked against the site you are booked on. We never track your location at any other time. Full details are in the Location & privacy notice and Data Protection Impact Assessment inside NicoNet.
  • Work records: jobs allocated to you, updates and photographs of your work, materials and receipts, van checks, keys and tools, timesheets and work orders.
  • Requests and messages: holidays, sickness and return-to-work records, 1-2-1 and appraisal requests, uniform and PPE, messages to the office, and messages with residents about a repair (first names only).
  • Safety and compliance: induction and RAMS sign-offs, accident and near-miss reports, training and tickets, vehicle declarations and right to work evidence.
  • Documents: contracts, new starter forms, payslips and P60s made available to you.
  • Technical information: sign-in records, the one-time codes we email you, and, if you allow notifications, a device token so we can send you alerts. We do not use advertising identifiers, trackers or third-party analytics.

Sickness and accident records can include health information. We use it only to manage absence, pay, health and safety and our legal duties as an employer.

4. Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To run your contract or agreement, including pay, holidays and work allocation (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To meet our legal obligations, including health and safety, right to work, working time, tax and pensions (Article 6(1)(c)), and, for health information, Article 9(2)(b) and the Data Protection Act 2018, Schedule 1, paragraph 1.
  • For our legitimate interests in accurate attendance records, site safety and roll call, running an efficient maintenance service and keeping a record of communications with residents (Article 6(1)(f)).

5. Who sees it

You can see your own records. Paul Nicholson, Kevin Sharp and Callum Hesketh can see staff records needed for their role; confidential items such as grievances go only to the people they are addressed to. We use these service providers, who act on our instructions: Supabase (database and sign-in, London region), Microsoft 365 (email and calendars), Expo, Apple and Google (app delivery and notifications), Anthropic (Claude, which drafts and polishes messages; message text is processed only to return the wording and is not used to train models) and GitHub (hosting of the web versions). We do not sell personal information.

6. How long we keep it

Clock-in location readings are deleted 12 months after they are taken. Other staff records are kept for the length of your employment or engagement and then for the periods in the staff handbook retention schedule, usually six years after you leave, so that we can meet legal, tax and pension obligations and deal with any claims.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to restrict or stop using it, object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and ask for a copy of information you gave us in a portable format. Contact us as set out in section 1. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

8. Changes to this notice

We will tell you in NicoNet if we change this notice in a way that affects you.
