Help and support

Nicholson Living™: the tenant app from Nicholson Lettings

Emergency? If you smell gas, call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. For a power cut, call 105. If there is a fire or any danger to life, call 999. Then report it as an Emergency repair in the app.

Getting help with your tenancy

Once you are signed in, use Messages or Report a repair in the app. Everything you send is logged against your tenancy and answered by the Nicholson Lettings team.

How to use the app

The Nicholson Living resident guide (PDF) walks you through signing in, repairs, emergencies, your rent account, documents, extras and requests. You can also open it in the app from Account.

Signing in

  • Enter the email address you gave us for your tenancy. We email you a 6-digit code. There is no password to remember.
  • No code? Check your junk or spam folder. Codes come from office@nicholsongroup.co.uk.
  • If the app says it can't find your tenancy, we may hold a different email address for you.

If you still can't sign in, email office@nicholsongroup.co.uk with your name and address.
